The game

AMUSE is a location based collection game. You explore real places, collect profession agents, and grow their mastery by using them. The game runs on your real location only. Safety

Never play while driving. The game pauses scanning at road speed; passengers may attest and play. Play only in public places or places you have permission to be. Never trespass. Depots sit on public map data; appearing on the map is not permission to enter private property. Business owners and residents may request removal of a location via the support page, and removals are honored.
