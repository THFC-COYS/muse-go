Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-28. AMUSE (working name) is a location based collection game developed by Greg Lucas, Phoenix, Arizona (the "developer"). This policy describes the beta build honestly, including what it does not do.

Location

Location is the game. With your permission, the app reads your device's GPS position to place you on the map, find real places near you, spawn agents, and operate Agent Depots and Forts. In the web beta, your coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap's Overpass service (and its public mirrors) solely to look up nearby named places, and map tiles load from OpenStreetMap servers. The developer operates no server that receives or stores your location in the beta. Your precise location is never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never used for tracking across other apps or websites.

If you deny location permission, the game cannot run. That is by design, and the game says so rather than quietly degrading.

Data stored on your device

Your collection, currency balances, settings, walking distance totals, and purchase records are stored locally on your device (browser local storage in the web beta, app storage in the native build). Clearing your browser data or deleting the app erases this data. The beta offers a backup code you control; the developer never sees it unless you send it.

Purchases

In the native app, purchases are processed by Apple or Google through in-app purchase. The developer receives purchase confirmations (what was bought, not your payment details) from the store or its payments processor to deliver and restore items. The web beta's store runs in a labeled sandbox: no real payment is collected and no payment information is requested.

Accounts

The beta has no accounts and no sign-in. When accounts arrive in a later version, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and signing in will be optional for core play.

Children

AMUSE is not directed at children under 13, and Depots are never placed at K-12 schools. Parents who believe a child has used the game may contact the developer using the route on the support page.

Changes and contact
