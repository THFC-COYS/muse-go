Fourteen launch species. Every one wears the Muse family look, the navy figure with the glowing amber core, dressed for its profession so you can read it at a glance. 4. The Shop: two currencies, honest rules

Embers are earned by playing: catching agents, running real tasks, keeping a daily streak. Core Coins are the premium currency, bought with real money in the native app. The guardrail that never bends: money speeds things up, it never buys mastery or trust. Capability is earned by using your agents. Every price below is stated in plain language, and there are no loot boxes and no hidden odds anywhere in this game.

Place weighting multiplies a species' base rate on its home turf (about 6x where its real places surround you, still possible elsewhere at a trickle), and the turf is read from your actual GPS position and the mapped places near it. There is no manual override, ever. Time of day adds flavor: Chefs surge at meal times, Pilots at flight banks, Trainers at dawn and dusk. You get 3 Muse Orbs per ordinary encounter; a wild agent can break free and flee. Epic and Legendary spawns are Masters: orbs do not work on them. The honest loop

Explore, collect, delegate, watch it work, rate the result. Agents get better because you use them on real work, so the game rewards the habit Muse is actually for. The Dex tracks species, your best mastery per species, and a Hall of Receipts: the real outcomes your crew produced.

Governance is the game

Every agent has a named owner: you. Mastery raises autonomy, but the hard floor never moves: send, pay, delete, and publish stay behind your approval at every level, and every autonomous run ends in a receipt you can audit or revoke.

The name: AMUSE

Chosen by Greg 2026-10-28, and it is a wink hiding in plain sight: A-MUSE, the Muse glowing inside an ordinary word for delight that nobody can fence off. It backronyms the whole game: Agents, Mastery, Upgrades, Skills, Exploration. Store title: "AMUSE: Collect AI Agents." Held in reserve: MuSeum and Agentia. Retired on collision checks: Pocket Agents and Agent Atlas, and any title built directly on Muse or GO, which belong to other people's trademarks. A formal trademark search still runs before store submission.

⚔️ Master Trials and 🛡️ Forts (the battle system)

Trials: Epic and Legendary wilds are Master agents and cannot be caught with orbs. You nominate a champion from your crew; its unlocked real tasks are its moves (power = task XP), and its Resolve is 40 + 12 per mastery level. The Master answers with signature work of its own. Family matchups matter (Care outplays Spirit, Spirit outplays Knowledge, Knowledge outplays Trades, Trades outplays Commerce, Commerce outplays Travel, Travel outplays Food, Food outplays Care), and a champion on its home turf hits 10% harder. Win and the Master joins you, the only way Masters are ever earned. Lose and it returns to the wild while your champion banks +10 training XP: standing in is never wasted. Forts: named real places on your map are Agent Depots. Walk within 100 m to spin for supplies (Embers, beacons, XP charges, Focus Snacks), and station your strongest agent there to hold the fort: it earns +8 mastery XP and +5 Embers per hour (capped at 8 hours per stint) while you explore, but it cannot run tasks until recalled. One fort at a time. Both systems run on the same law as everything else here: real location, real work, receipts for everything.

The economy, designed honestly

Earn (Embers): catch an agent +20 (rarity bonus up to +100), run a real task +15, rate a task genuinely useful +10, daily streak +50. Spend: beacons, storage, boosts, and cosmetics are the sinks, tuned so a free player earns a beacon roughly every two days of real play. Premium (Core Coins): the same shop accepts Coins for players who would rather accelerate, plus Coin only cosmetics and the Season Pass. Beyond currency: sponsored locations (a cafe or clinic pays to be a spawn hotspot, always labeled "Sponsored"), the Pro tier, and cosmetic bundles. The funnel, plainly: a free player gets every species, every mastery level, and every real task, forever. Paying buys speed, space, and style. It never buys mastery XP outright, never raises an agent's autonomy tier, and never skips an approval gate. No gambling mechanics: where chance exists (wild spawns), the odds are published in the design section of this page. Family safe, regulator clean, prices in plain language.

Currency names, settled: soft currency is Embers (earned, never bought). Premium currency is Core Coins, named for the glowing core every agent carries, and deliberately not named after anyone else's product.

From web proof to native app

Real in this v1

Real device geolocation (with your permission), the full place weighted spawn engine, rarity and catch math, the 14 species Dex, mastery XP and levels, task runs with receipts, your collection saved on this device, and a copy ready Muse brief for every agent you own.

Stubbed or simulated in v1

Place detection is real and now mandatory in spirit: the game runs on device location alone, reads actual OpenStreetMap places around your GPS fix, and sets turf and depots from them (Google Places is the production upgrade, and it needs an API key). There is deliberately no manual place picker and no override: that is Greg's anti couch-surfing rule. Task outputs are simulated in game; the exported brief is what makes the agent real inside your own Muse. Payments run through a store bridge: honest sandbox on the web, Apple and Google in-app purchase in the native build. No accounts yet, no server, no background tracking; your collection lives on this device, with a backup code as the safety net.

The full build (underway)

The beta ships as a Capacitor app: this exact game, wrapped natively for TestFlight and Google Play closed testing, with the store bridge switched to real in-app purchases and the policies (privacy, terms, location removal) published alongside. Behind it, the production v1 in React Native + Expo adds accounts with cloud saves and server checked spawns, background alerts when a rare agent is near, and Google Places data. The beta already carries the production safety rules: K-12 schools never host Depots, a driving lockout pauses scanning at road speed, and teleport jumps earn no walking credit. Full plan: LAUNCH-PLAN.md in the project repo.

Muse integration, honestly

Today the bridge is the brief: collect an agent, copy its WHO / WHAT / WHERE YOU STAY IN CHARGE brief into Muse, and it works there. A one tap "send to my Muse" needs a Muse provisioning API that does not exist yet; when it lands, the Dex becomes the front door to your whole crew.
