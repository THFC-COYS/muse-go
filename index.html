Play safe, always: agents only spawn in public places. Never trespass, never play while driving, and watch the real world, not just the screen. If a place is closed, private, or feels wrong, the agent is not worth it. There will be another one.

Today's Quests

Three fresh quests every day. Finish them by actually playing: catching, working, exploring your real neighborhood. Claim the Embers when the bar fills.

🛡️ Your Fort
No agent stationed. Open a Depot on the map (a real landmark near you) and station your strongest agent there: it holds the ground, earns mastery XP and Embers while you are away, and comes home stronger when you recall it. A stationed agent cannot run tasks until recalled, and only one agent can hold a fort at a time.

2. The Agent Dex

Fourteen launch species. Every one wears the Muse family look, the navy figure with the glowing amber core, dressed for its profession so you can read it at a glance. Greyed out means not yet collected. Tap any card, or any wild agent on the map, to see its full profile: the tasks it can accomplish for you, what each mastery level unlocks, and where it lives.

3. My Crew: put them to work

Collected agents earn mastery XP by completing real tasks for you, never by grinding. Higher mastery unlocks bigger, more autonomous work, and every run leaves a receipt. In v1 the tasks are simulated in game; the brief each agent exports is the real thing you paste into Muse.

4. The Shop: two currencies, honest rules

Embers are earned by playing: catching agents, running real tasks, keeping a daily streak. Muse Coins are the premium currency, bought with real money in the native app. The guardrail that never bends: money speeds things up, it never buys mastery or trust. Capability is earned by using your agents. Every price below is stated in plain language, and there are no loot boxes and no hidden odds anywhere in this game.

Your wallet: 0 Embers · 0 Muse Coins

Place weighting multiplies a species' base rate on its home turf (about 6x where its real places surround you, still possible elsewhere at a trickle), and the turf is read from your actual GPS position and the mapped places near it. There is no manual override, ever. Time of day adds flavor: Chefs surge at meal times, Pilots at flight banks, Trainers at dawn and dusk. You get 3 Muse Orbs per ordinary encounter; a wild agent can break free and flee. Epic and Legendary spawns are Masters: orbs do not work on them. Your champion must beat them in a Trial of Work (below), and landmarks near you double as Agent Depots where you spin for supplies and station your strongest agent to hold a fort.

The honest loop

Explore, collect, delegate, watch it work, rate the result. Agents get better because you use them on real work, so the game rewards the habit Muse is actually for. The Dex tracks species, your best mastery per species, and a Hall of Receipts: the real outcomes your crew produced.

Governance is the game

Every agent has a named owner: you. Mastery raises autonomy, but the hard floor never moves: send, pay, delete, and publish stay behind your approval at every level, and every autonomous run ends in a receipt you can audit or revoke.

⚔️ Master Trials and 🛡️ Forts (the battle system)

Trials: Epic and Legendary wilds are Master agents and cannot be caught with orbs. You nominate a champion from your crew; its unlocked real tasks are its moves (power = task XP), and its Resolve is 40 + 12 per mastery level. The Master answers with signature work of its own. Family matchups matter (Care outplays Spirit, Spirit outplays Knowledge, Knowledge outplays Trades, Trades outplays Commerce, Commerce outplays Travel, Travel outplays Food, Food outplays Care), and a champion on its home turf hits 10% harder. Win and the Master joins you, the only way Masters are ever earned. Lose and it returns to the wild while your champion banks +10 training XP: standing in is never wasted. Forts: named real places on your map are Agent Depots. Walk within 100 m to spin for supplies (Embers, beacons, XP charges, Focus Snacks), and station your strongest agent there to hold the fort: it earns +8 mastery XP and +5 Embers per hour (capped at 8 hours per stint) while you explore, but it cannot run tasks until recalled. One fort at a time. Both systems run on the same law as everything else here: real location, real work, receipts for everything.

The economy, designed honestly

Earn (Embers): catch an agent +20 (rarity bonus up to +100), run a real task +15, rate a task genuinely useful +10, daily streak +50. Spend: beacons, storage, boosts, and cosmetics are the sinks, tuned so a free player earns a beacon roughly every two days of real play. Premium (Muse Coins): the same shop accepts Coins for players who would rather accelerate, plus Coin only cosmetics and the Season Pass. Beyond currency: sponsored locations (a cafe or clinic pays to be a spawn hotspot, always labeled "Sponsored"), the Pro tier, and cosmetic bundles. The funnel, plainly: a free player gets every species, every mastery level, and every real task, forever. Paying buys speed, space, and style. It never buys mastery XP outright, never raises an agent's autonomy tier, and never skips an approval gate. No gambling mechanics: where chance exists (wild spawns), the odds are published in the design section of this page. Family safe, regulator clean, prices in plain language.

Currency name candidates (Greg's pick): soft: Embers (this build), Sparks, Glow Drops. Premium: Muse Coins (this build), Core Coins, Aurics.

From web proof to native app

Real in this v1

Real device geolocation (with your permission), the full place weighted spawn engine, rarity and catch math, the 14 species Dex, mastery XP and levels, task runs with receipts, your collection saved on this device, and a copy ready Muse brief for every agent you own.

Stubbed or simulated in v1

Place detection is real and now mandatory in spirit: the game runs on device location alone, reads actual OpenStreetMap places around your GPS fix, and sets turf and depots from them (Google Places is the production upgrade, and it needs an API key). There is deliberately no manual place picker and no override: that is Greg's anti couch-surfing rule. Task outputs are simulated in game; the exported brief is what makes the agent real inside your own Muse. No accounts, no server, no background tracking.

Native v2 (iOS + Android, one codebase)

React Native + Expo in TypeScript (the web build's catalog, spawn tables, and progression logic carry over as shared modules), a real map with live place categories (Google Places primary, OpenStreetMap fallback; the web prototype can read OpenStreetMap place tags), accounts with server checked spawns, alerts when a rare agent is near, optional camera AR encounters with a fully playable non AR mode, walking friendly spawn points vetted as public, and team collections. One codebase, both stores. One more honest web limit: clearing this browser's data erases a web collection; accounts fix that in v2.

Muse integration, honestly

Today the bridge is the brief: collect an agent, copy its WHO / WHAT / WHERE YOU STAY IN CHARGE brief into Muse, and it works there. A one tap "send to my Muse" needs a Muse provisioning API that does not exist yet; when it lands, the Dex becomes the front door to your whole crew.
