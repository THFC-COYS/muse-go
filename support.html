Support

The game says it cannot run without location

That is the design, not a bug. Agentia runs on your real location alone. Check that location services are on for your browser or the app, and that you are outdoors or near a window for the first fix.

I lost my collection

In the beta your game lives on your device. If you saved a backup code (Your Data card, in the Quests section of the game), paste it back with Restore. Without a code, a cleared browser or deleted app cannot be recovered; accounts with cloud saves arrive in a later version.

Purchases

Web beta purchases are sandbox simulations and never charge you. In the native app, purchases run through Apple or Google; use Restore Purchases in the Shop if an item does not appear, and contact the store for refunds.

Request removal of a location

If your home, business, school, or place of worship appears as an Agent Depot and you want it removed, contact the developer through aiccountable.io with the place name and address as shown on the map. Removals are honored within 7 days by blocklisting the location, regardless of map data updates. Depot place data comes from OpenStreetMap; you may also correct the underlying data there.

Report a safety issue or a bug

Same route: aiccountable.io. Say what you saw, where (the place name on the map), and which build you are playing. Safety reports are read first.
